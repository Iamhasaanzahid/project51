Privacy policy.

The short version: there are no accounts, no analytics and no stored uploads. This page explains exactly what happens to an image when you use the studio.

Summary

  • No account, no login, no profile.
  • No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics scripts.
  • The 3D studio processes images entirely on your device.
  • The AI studio sends your photo to a Cloudflare Worker only for the duration of the render. It is not written to a database by us.
  • Recent renders are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device, until you clear them.

In your browser

The 3D room loads avatar and fabric data and renders it with WebGL. Your uploaded textile photo is read with the browser's FileReader API and used to build a texture in memory. It is never transmitted. Closing the tab discards it.

The site is served as static files. Hosting providers may keep standard request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention, as any web server does.

When you use the AI studio

If you press Generate with AI, the following is sent over HTTPS to the MHZALY Cloudflare Worker:

  • your uploaded image, if you selected one;
  • the prompt text you typed;
  • the item category you picked and the render mode.

The Worker forwards the request to an AI image model to produce the result, then returns the image to your browser. We do not store the request or the result in a database, and there is no user account to attach it to.

AI providers process requests under their own terms and retention policies. Where a provider states that it does not retain inputs for training on the free tier, that provider's policy applies.

Local storage on your device

The site uses browser storage for functionality only:

  • mhzaly_gen_mode — remembers your last render mode.
  • mhzaly_ai_dead — a short-lived flag so the app stops retrying a quota-limited service.
  • mhzaly_history — thumbnails of your recent renders, so you can re-download them.
  • mhzaly_backend — an optional custom backend URL you may enter yourself.
  • Provider API keys — only if you choose to paste your own keys. They stay in this browser and are never sent to us.

You can clear all of this at any time from your browser's site-data settings. There is no server-side copy to delete.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare — static hosting and the serverless AI Worker.
  • AI model providers — the image and vision models used to produce a render.
  • Google Gemini — only if you supply your own Gemini key in the advanced settings.

No advertising, profiling or data-broker services are used.

Your choices

Because there are no accounts and no stored uploads, there is no personal profile to export or delete. To remove local data, clear site data for this domain. To avoid sending an image to an AI provider entirely, use the 3D room or the Same photo mode, which run locally.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of the page will change and the new text will be published here. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
