Why it works

Fidelity first, then speed

The hard part is not generating an image. It is keeping the item the same.

01

Colour measured, not guessed

Vision models misname colour constantly. The studio strips colour words from the description and samples the centre of your photo instead, then locks the real palette into the prompt.

02

Reference-guided design lock

In Best AI mode your photo is sent to the model as a visual reference, so the silhouette, print and hardware stay anchored to the original.

03

Your words, verbatim

If you type a prompt, it is used exactly as written — no silent rewriting, no “enhancement” that changes the brief.

04

No account, no card

There is no sign-up, no trial timer and no payment page. Open the studio and render.

05

Fast where it matters

A design-locked render typically lands in 5–15 seconds. Re-rolling a variation reuses the same brief and only regenerates the frame.

06

Always something to download

If the AI allowance is spent, the studio falls back to a free renderer, and finally to a pixel-locked studio cut-out of your exact item.
