1. The service

The Cloth Room is a free, browser-based virtual try-on studio operated by MHZALY. It has two parts: a local 3D room that runs on your device, and an AI studio that sends a request to a serverless backend to generate an image.

There is no account system, no subscription and no paid plan. We may change, pause or retire any part of the service at any time, including the free AI allowance.
