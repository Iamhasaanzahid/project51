    Shoulder, chest, waist and hem are read from the avatar, so a kurta falls to the knee instead of floating at the chest.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Is my photo or fabric uploaded anywhere?

    In the 3D room, no — images are read in your browser, used to build a texture, and discarded when you close the tab. In the AI studio, a photo you choose to render is sent to the render service for that one request only and is not stored. There is no account either way.

    Which garments can I try?

    Men: kurta, pathani, shalwar kameez, sherwani, bandhgala, blazer, overshirt and waistcoat. Women: kurti, kurta, shalwar kameez, anarkali, lehenga, saree, dress and sharara — plus separate bottoms and fits.

    Can I use my own fabric photo?

    Yes. Drop a clear, evenly lit photo of the textile — a weave close-up or a flat-lay works best. The colour, print and texture are lifted onto the 3D cut.

    How do I keep a look?

    Press Save PNG to composite the model, garment and scene into one image. Saved looks stay in My Looks for the session and can be downloaded again.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes. The studio is responsive, and the 3D engine runs on modern mobile browsers. A recent phone with a decent GPU gives the smoothest rotation.
