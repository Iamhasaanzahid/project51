Features

✕

No Phone Number

Sign up with email only. System auto-assigns a UIN (Unique Identity Number).

End-to-End Encrypted

X25519 ECDH + AES-256-GCM + HKDF-SHA256. Server stores only ciphertext.

📡

Radio Mesh

Works over cellular SMS, Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi Direct. No internet required.

📱

Media Sharing

Share images, voice notes and files. Encrypted end-to-end before leaving your device.

How It Works

1

Sign Up

Enter email. System assigns UIN (+92-MZ-NNNNNN). No phone number needed.

2

Connect

Choose radio: Bluetooth (nearby), Wi-Fi Direct (local), or Cellular (SMS).

3

Message

Type text or share images. E2EE encryption. Sent over chosen radio mesh.

4

Mesh Relay

Messages hop phone-to-phone. TTL=5 hops extends range beyond direct range.
